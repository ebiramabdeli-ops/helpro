## 4. Trust & Verification Intelligence

**The platform enforces REAL trust, not fake ratings.**

### Verification Includes:
- 🆔 Identity verification (third-party: Veriff, Onfido)
- 🎂 Age verification (18+)
- 📍 Address/location validation (proof of residence)
- 📜 License checks (where required: electrician, plumber)

### Trust Signals:
- ✅ Job completion history (completion rate > 90%)
- ⏰ Punctuality (on-time arrival > 85%)
- ⭐ Customer ratings (average > 4.0)
- 🤝 Dispute-free record (disputes < 5%)

### Trust Scoring Formula:
```
TrustScore = (
  Base: 50
  + EmailVerified: +5
  + PhoneVerified: +5
  + IdentityVerified: +15
  + BackgroundCheck: +25
  + AvgRating * 4 (max +20)
  + CompletionRate * 0.15 (max +15)
  + AccountAge (months) * 0.5 (max +10)
  - Disputes * 5
) clamped to [0, 100]
```

### Trust Gates:
- **Low Trust (< 50)**: Limited job access, lower earnings, require deposit
- **Medium Trust (50-75)**: Standard jobs, normal earnings
- **High Trust (75-90)**: Premium jobs, priority matching, higher earnings
- **Elite Trust (90+)**: B2B access, corporate clients, VIP treatment

## 6. Intelligence Behavior (Non-LLM Friendly)

**The AI / system must:**

### Ask Structured Questions, NOT Open Chat
❌ Bad: "Tell me what you need"  
✅ Good: "What do you need? [Cleaning] [Moving] [Recycling]"

### Guide Users Step-by-Step
✅ Step 1: Select service  
✅ Step 2: Enter location  
✅ Step 3: Choose date/time  
✅ Step 4: Review & confirm  

### Reduce Mistakes in Job Requests
- ✅ Pre-fill common options
- ✅ Show price estimates immediately
- ✅ Validate inputs (address, date, time)
- ✅ Warn about peak pricing (weekends, holidays)

### Prevent Illegal or Unsafe Jobs
- ❌ Block: "Install gas line" (requires certified professional)
- ❌ Block: "Dispose of chemicals" (illegal)
- ❌ Block: "Structural renovation" (licensed contractor needed)
- ✅ Suggest alternatives when possible

### Optimize Matching Based On:
1. **Location** (distance < 10km preferred)
2. **Availability** (provider schedule matches)
3. **Skills** (service type matches provider experience)
4. **Trust score** (higher trust = priority)
5. **Cost efficiency** (fair price for customer AND provider)

**Matching Algorithm (Rule-Based):**
```
Score = (
  Distance: (1 - distance/20km) * 30 points
  + Availability: hasTimeSlot ? 25 : 0
  + TrustScore: (trustScore/100) * 20 points
  + Rating: (rating/5) * 15 points
  + CompletionRate: completionRate * 10 points
) max 100 points
```

**The intelligence is DECISION-DRIVEN, not CONVERSATION-DRIVEN.**

## 8. Disputes & Safety

### System Principles:
- ⚖️ Neutral platform (not biased toward customer OR provider)
- 📸 Evidence-based resolution (photos, messages, timestamps)
- 🔒 Fair outcomes (refund if justified, payment if work done)

### Dispute Flow:

**1. User Reports Issue**
- Customer: "Service not completed"
- Provider: "Customer not home"

**2. Evidence Collection**
- Photos (before/after work)
- Messages (communication log)
- GPS timestamp (proof of arrival)

**3. Temporary Freeze**
- Payment held in escrow
- Both parties notified
- 48h response window

**4. Human Review**
- Support team reviews evidence
- Contact both parties if needed
- Make decision based on facts

**5. Resolution**
- ✅ Work completed as agreed → Release payment to provider
- ❌ Work not done → Full refund to customer
- ⚖️ Partial work → Partial payment (negotiated)

### Dispute Prevention:
- ✅ Clear job descriptions (scope definition)
- ✅ Photos before/after (proof of work)
- ✅ In-app messaging (evidence trail)
- ✅ GPS check-in (provider arrival time)
